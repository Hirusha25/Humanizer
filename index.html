The score is a heuristic built from the signals public detectors are known to weigh: sentence-length variation, formal connectors, AI-tell vocabulary, contraction rate and em dashes. It is a guide for editing, not a copy of GPTZero, Turnitin or Originality. Always read the result before you use it.

How it works

Kills the tells

Hundreds of phrases that language models over-use (“delve”, “tapestry”, “it is important to note”, “in today’s fast-paced world”) are swapped for plain alternatives. Em dashes and semicolons become commas or full stops.

Breaks the rhythm

Detectors flag text where every sentence is the same length. Long sentences get split at natural clause boundaries, short ones get joined, and some clauses are flipped around.

Sounds like a person

Contractions, dropped throat-clearing (“Furthermore, …”), and near-synonym swaps make the word choice less predictable, which is exactly what perplexity-based detectors measure.

Private by design

No API keys, no accounts, no uploads. The engine is a few hundred lines of JavaScript that runs in this tab. It also works from the command line.
